# 🔍 Analytical Approach

For most projects, I follow a structured analytical process:

**1. Understand the Business Problem**

Define the objective and identify the questions the analysis needs to answer.

**2. Data Preparation**

Inspect the dataset for:

* Missing values
* Duplicates
* Incorrect data types
* Inconsistent formatting
* Invalid records
* Potential outliers

**3. Exploratory Data Analysis**

Explore distributions, relationships, trends, patterns, and important segments.

**4. KPI Development**

Develop relevant metrics to measure business performance.

**5. Visualization**

Create dashboards and charts that communicate important findings clearly.

**6. Business Insights**

Translate analytical findings into understandable business insights.

**7. Recommendations**

Provide practical recommendations based on the evidence in the data.
